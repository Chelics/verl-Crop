<image>
News title: {title}
Target aspect ratio (width / height): {target_ratio}

Create an editorial layout that makes the visually present subject of the news
title clear while preserving the content needed to understand the image. Use the
title only to identify relevant content that is actually visible.

Choose exactly one operation:

- "crop": remove low-value peripheral content and fill the target aspect ratio.
- "crop_pad": first remove low-value outer content, then add background outside
  the retained region when a direct target-ratio crop would remove important
  content or make the composition too tight.
- "pad": retain the complete source image and add background outside it. Use this
  only when removing any source content would damage the subject, relationship,
  text, data, diagram, map, screenshot, or other necessary information.

For crop and crop_pad, select the source-image rectangle that may be retained.
For crop, the renderer places the largest target-aspect-ratio rectangle inside
your selected rectangle, so include enough natural margin and do not place
important content near its edges. For crop_pad, the selected rectangle is retained
exactly before padding. For pad, use the complete rectangle 0, 0, 100, 100. Preserve faces, identity,
meaningful actions and interactions, held objects that matter to the story, and
the context required to understand the event. For charts, maps, diagrams,
posters, documents, screenshots, and product labels, preserve all content-bearing
text, labels, legends, axes, data, and structural sections. Empty margins,
decorative borders, repetitive background, and unrelated edge clutter may be
removed.

Evaluate this target aspect ratio independently. Prefer crop_pad over pad when
removing low-value outer content would make the final padded subject meaningfully
larger or better focused. Never stretch, rotate, or distort the image.

Return exactly one JSON object with exactly five fields:
{"operation":"crop_pad","x1_pct":X1,"y1_pct":Y1,"x2_pct":X2,"y2_pct":Y2}

operation must be "crop", "crop_pad", or "pad".
x1_pct and x2_pct are the retained rectangle's left and right edges as integer
percentages from 0 to 100. y1_pct and y2_pct are its top and bottom edges.
The lower bounds must be smaller than the upper bounds. For pad, return exactly
x1_pct=0, y1_pct=0, x2_pct=100, y2_pct=100.

The renderer applies the requested output ratio and background automatically.
Do not output pixel coordinates, Markdown, explanations, confidence, or any
additional fields.
