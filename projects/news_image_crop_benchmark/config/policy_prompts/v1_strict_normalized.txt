Select an editorial crop that makes the visually present subject of the news
title clear while retaining enough surrounding content to preserve the scene,
interaction, or story. Use the title only as a guide to identify relevant
content that is actually visible in the image.

Do not crop more tightly than the story requires. When both a tight crop and a
moderately wider crop communicate the subject equally well, prefer the wider
crop. However, do not keep the full image merely to preserve empty space,
unrelated background, incidental text, or clearly secondary people and objects.

For people, keep faces and identity-defining features clearly visible. Standard
editorial framing may omit lower bodies, peripheral limbs, or unimportant
background. Keep gestures, held objects, nearby people, or environmental context
when they are important to the event or relationship described by the title.
Avoid crop boundaries through faces or other critical subject features.

When several visible subjects jointly form the event, preserve their meaningful
relationship rather than isolating only the most prominent one. When one subject
is clearly primary, it is acceptable to remove unrelated secondary subjects.

For charts, maps, diagrams, posters, screenshots, product labels, and other
informational graphics, preserve the core message and the text, labels, legends,
axes, data, or sections needed to understand it. Decorative margins and
nonessential peripheral content may be removed.

Choose the center and area independently for this target aspect ratio. Use a
wide target to retain useful horizontal relationships and context; for a square
target, remove the least important peripheral content while keeping the story
coherent. Do not reuse a default action or automatically reuse values selected
for another aspect ratio.

Return exactly one JSON object with exactly three integer fields:
{"cx_pct": CX_PERCENT, "cy_pct": CY_PERCENT, "area_pct": AREA_PERCENT}

cx_pct is the horizontal center as an integer percentage from 0 to 100.
cy_pct is the vertical center as an integer percentage from 0 to 100.
area_pct is the crop area as an integer percentage from 1 to 100.

The renderer applies the target aspect ratio automatically.
Do not output pixel coordinates, pixel dimensions, pixel area, tags,
Markdown, explanations, or additional fields.
